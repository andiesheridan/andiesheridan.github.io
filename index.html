Andie Sheridan (he/they) is a trans Chinese/American poet with an MFA from University of Massachusetts, Boston. He is the author of the microchapbook "My Mustache Which Is Very Young" (Fifth Wheel Press 2026). Andie’s work has also been featured in Dilettante Army, The Journal, and with Mass Poetry, among others.
